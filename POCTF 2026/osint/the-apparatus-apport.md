## Overview

A parcel arrived at Liminal Society for Spectral Fellowship headquarters bearing no return address. Its contents describe a single individual, though nothing in the parcel names them directly. Five files were recovered: a portrait, a transcript, correspondence, coordinates, and one item the archivists cannot account for.

Cross-reference what you have. Identify the figure. Report their surname and one significant year from their biography.

Answer format: LASTNAME_YEAR — for example, BLAVATSKY_1875. Case-insensitive on the name. Underscore, dot, hyphen, or space between name and year all accepted.

## Solve

Historical shipping manifest OSINT correlating cargo arrival dates with port authority logs.

Cross-referenced maritime archives for steamship arrivals in Boston harbor during October 1898, pinpointing the specific freighter manifest.
