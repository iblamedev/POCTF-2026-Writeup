## Overview

A $1 bill came off the scanner in pieces. Reassemble it, then answer the question: **where am I?**

The bill has been places. Wherever it last left a record, so did the person who carried it. Find them. Find where they pointed the camera and what the camera saw.

## Solve

Multi-stage OSINT puzzle combining physical bill serial reconstruction, bill tracking registry, unindexed blog EXIF metadata, and celestial orientation.

Reassembled 30 shredded $1 bill fragments to get serial `L14828113A` Series 2017A, tracked user `whymark` on tracking site to find unindexed astrophotography blog, extracted camera EXIF data and observation coordinates to answer 'where am I?'.
