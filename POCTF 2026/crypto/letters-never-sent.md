## Overview

Here we have a letter that was recovered from the estate of Dr. H. Aldous Whitmore. It was never posted. 

Along with the letter, a strange message was discovered. We have no doubt that Dr. Whitmore meant to keep it secret, given his... Association with occult societies that was discovered after his death. Despite this, the relationship to the letter remains unclear, but a connection cannot be discounted. 

Find the key the letter hides, and read what Whitmore could not bring himself to send.

## Solve

Vigenère cipher embedded in an unsent handwritten letter with polyalphabetic key derivation from Victorian occult manuscript references.

Analyzing the transcript of the unsent letter revealed specific recurring capital letters that formed the key `WHITMORE`. Decrypting the ciphertext recovered Dr. Whitmore's confession.
