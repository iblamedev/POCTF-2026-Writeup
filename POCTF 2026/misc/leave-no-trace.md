## Overview

M. Lange's homepage has been abandoned.

The site looks harmless enough: a little outdated, a little forgotten, and apparently not worth anyone's attention anymore.

But websites have a habit of saying more than their authors intended.

If you're only looking at what's on the page, you're not really looking at the site.

Leave no trace. Find what was left behind.

## Solve

Linux memory forensics identifying process injection and wiped bash history artifacts.

Examined memory dump with Volatility, recovered injected hollow process code, and extracted memory buffers containing executed shellcommands.
