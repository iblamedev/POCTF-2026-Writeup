## Overview

A batch job has surfaced from the archives — two punchcards and a memo from Meridian Data Processing Services, 1967.

Someone thought this was an appropriate way to send you a message.

Read the memo. The clerk explains the format. Then study the cards.

## Solve

80-column IBM punched card encoding emulation from early mainframe data storage.

Mapped punched hole row positions (0-9, 11, 12 zone punches) according to the EBCDIC card code standard, decoding the card deck text.
