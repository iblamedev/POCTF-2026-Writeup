## Overview

VIENNA, NOVEMBER 1887.

An obscure chess column has turned up in the archives, documenting a correspondence game between two apparently respectable gentlemen.

At first glance, it's nothing remarkable.

Then you look at the moves.

Something about this particular game is very, very peculiar. Figure out what the players were actually doing, and you may discover that the game isn't quite about chess after all.

## Solve

Conway's Game of Life cellular automaton encoding message into initial grid states.

Simulated generations backwards using SAT solver (Z3) to deduce the original ancestor grid configuration that produced the stable pattern.
